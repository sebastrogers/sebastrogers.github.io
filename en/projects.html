Projects in detail

Applied research, artificial intelligence and solution development projects focused on public health, education and social impact.

VALERIA

Decision support application designed to assist health professionals in the differential clinical diagnosis of arboviruses such as Dengue, Chikungunya and Zika using machine learning models.

  • Area: artificial intelligence applied to health.
  • Group: dotLAB Brazil.
  • Access project

IAra

Platform focused on malaria epidemiological surveillance in Brazil's Legal Amazon, combining machine learning, spatial clustering and time series analysis.

  • Area: epidemiological surveillance and case prediction.
  • Group: dotLAB Brazil.
  • Access project

Hansen.Ai

dotLAB Brazil research project funded by CNPq, focused on investigating how artificial intelligence can support treatment and monitoring of people affected by leprosy.

  • Area: artificial intelligence, public health and neglected tropical diseases.
