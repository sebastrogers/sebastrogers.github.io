Notes on routine, studies and new paths

A space to record small changes, decisions and lessons outside the formal resume format.

Not everything that matters fits into a project list. Routine, discipline, doubts, discoveries and restarts are also part of professional life.

This blog can work as a public journal of growth: short, honest and useful texts, without needing to turn every update into a major announcement.

The goal is to write about what is being studied, which habits are helping and which questions remain open.
