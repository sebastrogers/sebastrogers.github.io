How I am organizing my professional projects

A draft method for turning experiences, ideas and experiments into a clearer portfolio.

Organizing projects is also organizing a narrative. It is not enough to say something was done; it matters to show which problem motivated the work, which choices were made and what changed afterwards.

My idea for this space is to keep simple records of ongoing projects, technical lessons, implementation decisions and results. This way, the portfolio stops being a static showcase and becomes a living record.

A good portfolio does not need to show everything. It needs to show enough for someone to understand how you work.

Over time, each project can have its own page with context, technologies, images, links and next steps.
