What I learned building this portfolio

Notes on clarity, visual consistency, internal pages and the importance of publishing a first version.

Building a portfolio means making decisions about identity. What appears on the first screen? What deserves its own page? What stays as a summary?

By separating the home page from the internal pages, the site gains two rhythms: a quick introduction for visitors arriving for the first time and detailed pages for those who want to understand the work more deeply.

  • The home page should be objective and invite exploration.
  • Internal pages should deepen the context.
  • The blog should record thinking, process and growth.

This structure can grow gradually, without needing to rebuild everything for every new publication.
