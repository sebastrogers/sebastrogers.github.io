Trajectory

I am a professor and researcher in Computing, connecting artificial intelligence, data science, technology development and teaching. My work is strongly related to projects applied to real-world problems, especially public health, neglected tropical diseases, epidemiological surveillance and computing education.

I am interested in turning research into useful technology: decision support systems, predictive models, data organization, scientific communication and the education of people who work with technology in a critical, practical and socially relevant way.

Experience

I work as a lecturer and researcher, connecting classrooms, research projects and applied initiatives with students, academic groups and institutional partners.

  • Researcher at dotLAB Brazil, working on artificial intelligence, data science and public health projects.
  • Teaching experience in Computing, Systems Development, Computer Networks, artificial intelligence and related areas.
  • Experience in academic coordination, projects, extension activities and professional training initiatives.
  • International experience associated with Dublin City University, with research in natural language processing and hate speech analysis in football.

Education

Ph.D. in Computer Engineering from the University of Pernambuco (POLI-UPE), M.Sc. in Informatics from the Institute of Computing at the Federal University of Alagoas (UFAL), specialization in Business Intelligence, Big Data and Analytics - Data Science, and a degree in Computing Education from the University of Pernambuco (UPE), Garanhuns campus.

Areas of work

My interests include Machine Learning, Deep Learning, MLOps, Text Mining, natural language processing, artificial intelligence applied to health, computing education, game development and games in education.

dotLAB Brazil

I work as a dotLAB Brazil researcher on projects that apply artificial intelligence and data science to public health problems. Related projects include VALERIA, Hansen.Ai and IAra, focused on decision support, epidemiological surveillance and health data analysis.
