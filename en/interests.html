Interests

Topics that guide my work

A closer view of the themes that connect my research, teaching practice and the projects I enjoy building.

Artificial intelligence applied to health

Use of machine learning, deep learning and data science to support epidemiological surveillance, public health, neglected tropical diseases, decision support and clinical and population data analysis.

Data science, MLOps and intelligent systems

Interest in the full lifecycle of data solutions: collection, curation, modeling, validation, delivery, monitoring and communication of results to technical and non-technical audiences.

Text mining, NLP and content analysis

Applications of text mining and natural language processing to understand discourse, identify patterns and turn large text collections into useful evidence.

Computing education

Teaching, mentoring, methodologies and technologies to educate people in computing, connecting theory, practice, research, extension and professional development.

Games, interaction and learning

Interest in game development, games in education and interactive experiences as paths for engagement, learning and creative experimentation.

Innovation, community and social impact

Projects that connect university, technology, students, communities and institutional partners, with a focus on practical outcomes, collaboration and social transformation.
